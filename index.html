Evidence behind each score

Reading Pone's score

Pone's reviews are good, and better than IDEX's or Swissbit's. The score is held back by things around the product: almost nobody writes about Pone, it holds only FIDO Level 1, and Finansavisen reported in December 2025 that money was running short. Visibility and certifications are the two fastest levers. Together they account for 29 of the 55 points Pone is missing.

Recommendations · ranked by impact on the next 24 months

What would move Pone forward

These recommendations draw on everything in the other tabs: public perception, the competitor comparison, the reputation score and the revenue risk model. Each one says what it is based on.

The situation in four lines

  • Pone has the only product among the four with a display, Bluetooth and a fingerprint sensor in a card. Reviewers like it.
  • It costs 1.6 to 6 times more than the competing products. It also has the weakest certifications, the lowest visibility and no customer that anyone outside Pone has confirmed.
  • Competition is closing in: IDEX, which supplies Pone's sensor, now sells its own FIDO2 card in Norway. Swissbit is previewing face biometrics, and CardLab and Token launched similar cards in 2026.
  • Two deadlines open a window: Microsoft switches off SMS and voice MFA in Entra on 1 February 2027, and Norway is preparing its NIS2 law.
