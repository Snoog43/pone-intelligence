Should you keep the salespeople?

Yes, but change what they do. With a €44.50 margin on a partner card, a salesperson costing about €100k a year has to drive about 2,250 partner cards a year just to pay for themselves. Selling small deals directly does not reach that. Large accounts and partner management do.

  • Stop selling deals under 50 cards directly. Route them to partners, who already earn 25% on them.
  • Pay on sell-out, not sell-in. Commission on cards actually activated by customers, so partners do not overstock.
  • Tier the partners. 25% discount as standard; 30% for partners above 500 cards a year; 20% for partners below 50. Put your time on the top 10.
  • Raise the deal size. At today's average order (roughly 10–25 cards per customer across 170 customers), €20M would need 7,000–18,000 new customers. At 400 cards per deal, it needs about 450.

Product & cost · built on Pone's sourcing file and 2026 market prices

Three tiers, and a card that costs half as much to make

This tab starts from Pone's own "Master Sourcing OFFPAD" file (September 2021, prices in SEK at 1k, 10k and 100k units), the €40 components + €28 manufacturing you gave, and a sweep of 2026 component, PCB and assembly prices plus about 35 competing products. Prices for the fingerprint sensor, the secure element and assembly are not public; those lines are estimates and need supplier quotes.

The short answer

  • OFFPAD+ is not overpriced for what it is. The only devices with fingerprint, Bluetooth and a display sell for about €170–240. The gap is elsewhere: Pone has nothing between €15 and €80, where most of the volume sells.
  • Its cost can fall from €68 to about €35 without removing a feature: a new PCB and display quote, a cheaper Bluetooth chip, and assembly in the Baltics or Poland instead of Sweden.
  • Add two cheaper tiers: a battery-free fingerprint card around €45 and a fingerprint + Bluetooth card without display around €79. Both keep a gross margin above 50% at partner prices.

Cost-down roadmap

Each new tier or a new chip needs fresh certification (FIDO, Bluetooth, CE/RED, FCC): about €80–150k per model, or €8–15 per card over 10,000 cards and about €1 over 100,000.

What it means for the €20M plan

Cheaper tiers need more cards for the same revenue, but they open the €30–80 segment where most buyers are, and they carry better margins than today's OFFPAD+. A mix keeps the high tier for admins and the mid tier for broad workforce rollouts.
